Packs & mods

Share your mods

Make your packs into a mod to share: one .zip with your quests, NPCs, enemies, world, content and scripts.
Got a mod from someone? Drop its .zip into the game's Mods folder. That's it: it's installed when the game or the editor next starts (or now, if this page is open), and its packs show up below, ready to play and to change like your own.

Packs

Packs are your changes. A pack that's switched off stays on disk (as .json.disabled) and the game ignores it. Changes load when the game starts (or in game with reloadquests, reloadcontent, npcreload in the F7 box).

Mods

Switching a mod off takes effect the next time the game starts. Install or remove mods with Setup.exe.
