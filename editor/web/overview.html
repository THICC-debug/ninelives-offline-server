Packs & mods

Packs

Packs are your changes. A pack that's switched off stays on disk (as .json.disabled) and the game ignores it. Changes load when the game starts (or in game with reloadquests, reloadcontent, npcreload in the F7 box).

Mods

Switching a mod off takes effect the next time the game starts. Install or remove mods with Setup.exe.

Made with AI (Anthropic's Claude) and tested by a human player. A fan project, not affiliated with SmokymonkeyS.
