# Revived Quests

Nine Lives has quests that are complete in its data but switched off, most of them because they never got
English text. This brings four of them back, with English translated from the original Japanese.

| Quest | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Cult Posters** | The Detective, Eastern Meadows | Find 10 Cult Posters and he asks where you got them (offered by itself once you have them). |
| **Cult Posters** (part 2) | The Detective, then Kedassi | Take the stack of posters he hands you to Kedassi, the Cult Hunter in the tavern. |
| **Maggie's Day** | Bakkstan, the lighthouse keeper, Eastern Meadows | Find an Onyx Stone and help clear Margaret's name. 120 gold, 1145 exp. |
| **The Blessed Road** (Phiremone's part) | Phiremone, the first Magus, Westpine Hollows | Where the main story stops ("to be continued"): Chaia LeFay sends you up the blessed road to Phiremone, who doubts you were ever in her Lodge and wants his slate from it. |
| **Phiremone's Slate** (new) | Chaia LeFay, Continental Highlands | After you've met Phiremone, Chaia hands you his slate to take back to him. |

Cult Posters never dropped anywhere in the original game, so undead (Bone Soldiers, Bone Archers and Bone
Mages) now drop them too; everything they dropped before still drops. Onyx Stones drop as they always did.

Phiremone's Slate had no source in the game at all, so **Phiremone's Slate** is a small new quest: Chaia
LeFay, whose Lodge it is, gives it to you (the same way Roffmore gives you the Butterfly Stick). Chaia's own
part of The Blessed Road is in the game already; it only gets the English it was missing for Phiremone's
reply when you reach him.

Text written for this mod (not in the original game): the new quest's lines, and Phiremone's thanks when you
bring the slate. Everything else is translated from the game's own Japanese.

## Install

Two packs, both needed:

- `revived-quests.json` goes in `Mods\Quests` (needs QuestLoader)
- `revived-quests-loot.json` goes in `Mods\Content` (needs ContentLoader)

Or turn them on in the Nine Lives Editor (Packs & mods). To undo, remove the two files: the quests go back
to switched off, and the undead to their original loot.
